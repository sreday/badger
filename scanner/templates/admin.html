How Snout works

  1. Every sponsor listed on an event's page gets a scanner link and its own 4-digit PIN, automatically. "Refresh Registered Sponsors" picks up new sponsors from the event pages straight away; it also runs by itself every 10 minutes.
  2. Send each sponsor their link and PIN. The whole booth team uses the same ones, each with their own name.
  3. Test until 6 hours before the event: everything works, and test scans disappear after an hour.
  4. Live from 6 hours before the start until 6 hours after the end.
  5. Closed after that: sponsors can still download or email their list for 90 days, then the leads are deleted.
